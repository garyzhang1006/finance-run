# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Kids around 8 to 12, playing alone on a laptop, tablet, or phone, often at home or in a classroom. They came to play a game, not to take a lesson, and they leave the moment it feels like homework.

## Product Purpose
Finance Run is a browser runner that teaches money basics (needs vs. wants, saving, interest, investing, borrowing, scams) through play. Success is a kid who replays it for fun and can explain afterward why saving first, avoiding impulse buys, and paying debt back quickly made their run go better.

## Brand Personality
Loud, bouncy, and fair. It should feel like a Saturday-morning arcade game: big colors, chunky type, sound on every action, and a win moment worth chasing. The money lessons speak plainly to a 10-year-old and never lecture.

## Anti-references
- Classroom quiz apps that wrap multiple-choice questions in a thin game skin.
- Grown-up fintech dashboards (navy, gray, small type, charts).
- Flat, static screens where nothing reacts to the player.

## Design Principles
- The mechanic teaches the lesson: money that sits in the wallet is at risk, saved money grows, borrowed money costs more.
- Every action gets feedback within a frame: a sound, a number popping, a particle.
- Short words, big numbers: any sentence a 10-year-old has to read twice is too long.
- Reward balance: the best result comes from saving and still having fun.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast for text, reduced-motion support, full keyboard and touch control, and no color-only signals for right or wrong.
